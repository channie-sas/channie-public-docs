# Channie Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Channie ("Channie," "the bot," "we," or "us") handles information when you install or use the Channie Discord application.

## Information we process

Channie may process the following information when it is needed for a feature you use:

- Discord identifiers, including user, server, channel, role, interaction, and message IDs.
- Discord account information made available to the bot, such as username, display name, avatar, and server membership or permissions relevant to a request.
- Commands, button and menu interactions, and message text or attachments sent as recognized `c`-prefixed commands, in configured feature channels, or during an active game or user session.
- Game and account state, including cards, inventory, credits, quests, settings, cooldowns, marketplace activity, rewards, event participation, and moderation or approval records.
- Card and DTI image submissions and the information required to review or display them.
- Discord-to-Minecraft link information and messages sent in an administrator-configured bridge channel.
- Limited technical and operational information needed to secure, diagnose, and maintain the service.

Channie's message router is limited to recognized commands, configured feature channels, and active sessions. Unrelated messages outside those contexts are ignored by application logic.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- provide Channie's collection, economy, marketplace, quest, profile, and multiplayer game features;
- process commands, free-form game answers, votes, uploads, and multi-step user flows;
- maintain game integrity, prevent duplicate processing or abuse, and enforce server or application rules;
- review card and image submissions, provide support, and investigate operational failures; and
- relay chat between a configured Discord channel and a linked Minecraft server when a server administrator enables that feature.

Message content is not sold or used for advertising or behavioral profiling.

## Storage and sharing

Channie stores persistent user and game state in PostgreSQL. Some uploads may be held temporarily while they are processed or reviewed. Completed or expired sessions and temporary files are removed through the service's normal cleanup processes.

When the Discord-to-Minecraft bridge is enabled by a server administrator, messages from the configured Discord channel are transmitted to the linked Minecraft server. Relayed messages may appear in the Minecraft server console and its normal server logs. Information may also be processed by Discord, hosting, database, backup, and infrastructure providers as needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably needed to operate Channie, preserve game and transaction integrity, secure the service, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. Removed data may remain in restricted backups or automatically rotated operational logs until those copies expire through their normal lifecycle.

To request access to or deletion of Channie data associated with your Discord account, join the [Channie Test Lab support server](https://discord.gg/MQS8nmeSCJ) and privately message the server owner. Do not post your Discord ID or other sensitive information in a public channel. We may ask you to verify control of the relevant Discord account before completing a request.

Deleting Channie's stored data does not automatically delete messages or files that remain on Discord or on a separately operated Minecraft server. Those records must be managed through the applicable Discord server or Minecraft server operator.

## Your choices

You may stop using Channie or remove it from a server at any time. Channie does not currently provide a separate opt-out while continuing to use message-content-dependent commands or games, because those features cannot operate without the message or attachment submitted for that interaction.

Server administrators can restrict the channels and permissions available to Channie and can disable the Discord-to-Minecraft bridge.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect stored information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Age requirements

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirements and any higher minimum age required in your country to use Channie.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Channie changes. Material updates will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, join the [Channie Test Lab support server](https://discord.gg/MQS8nmeSCJ) and privately message the server owner.
